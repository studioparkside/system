Every component on this page, rendered once, in the warm cream family already running across the Vert, Campminder brands, and Parallel Path proposals. Open it next to whatever you are building and match against it.

The token block and the base rules at the top of the stylesheet sit between START and END markers. To roll a change across every page, replace what is between the markers. Nothing outside them controls color, type scale, or spacing.

01
Foundations

Color, and where neutral belongs

Cream is the page. Pure white and pure black appear only where something is being judged, which is color swatches and reference art. Using them as page surfaces breaks the family.

Surfaces

Ratios measured against Card. Ink muted is the floor. Anything lighter fails AA at label sizes, which is where it would get used.

Accents
Gold#F9BC15 · ink on gold, 10.0:1
Blue#2278CE · white on blue, 4.5:1

Ink on gold

Ink on this gold is 10.0:1, the strongest pairing in the system. White on it is 1.8:1 and unreadable at any size, so gold takes ink only.

White on blue

White on this blue is 4.5:1, which clears AA with nothing to spare. Any lighter and body copy on blue stops being legal. Ink on it is 3.8:1, large text only.

One accent per page. Gold is doing the work on this one, marking the current step in the process row. Blue is held in reserve. The blue is as bright as it can go while white still clears AA on it, so treat #2278CE as the ceiling rather than a starting point.

White is a surface you can choose, unlike black. It reads as a clean break in a run of cream cards and works when a section is dense with reference art or data. Use it for one or two sections, never for the whole page, or the cream stops meaning anything.

Hairlines
hairline   rgba(28,27,24,0.16)   default rule
hairline-strong   rgba(28,27,24,0.85)   opens a set, sits above the first row
hairline-light   rgba(245,242,234,0.28)   rules inside an inverted card

Alpha, not hex. An alpha hairline sits correctly on any surface. A hex hairline only works on the one background it was mixed for, which is what breaks the moment a section inverts or a client theme swaps in.

Page type · shown on the white surface

Mood board

Same shell, three extra parts. Reference tiles, specimen blocks, and swatch rows. Everything a client is asked to judge sits on a neutral surface.

This whole section is running on white rather than cream, which is the case white earns. A page dense with reference art benefits from the cleaner ground, and the cream cards above and below still frame it.

Mood board shells run neutral, not cream. The client is there to judge their own material, so the page recedes rather than carrying Parkside warmth. Ground #F1F1F0, white cards, outline #E3E3E3, hairline #EAEAEA, soft ink #5C5C5C, muted #A5A5A5. Cream stays on proposals, where the client is judging Parkside. The neutral-surface rule already applied to tiles and swatches extends to the whole page. Boulder Events Calendar is the reference.

Reference tiles

Near-white, not cream, so the page ground does not tint the example. Numbered captions so a client can say "not 01-04" instead of describing it.

06
Structural copy

Language that repeats

These carry business consequence and should not be rewritten from scratch each time. Voice rules live in the writing style profile. These are the structures.

Before approving

This proposal is based on our conversation to date. Please carefully review the deliverables outlined below. If anything doesn't look right, let me know before approving.

Your project team

Please make sure everyone who approves direction is involved from the start. If someone new joins partway through and their input changes approved direction, that becomes a change in scope and I'll quote the adjustment before anything gets redone.

Project phases

Approving a phase closes it. The direction you approve carries forward into the phases that follow.

Worth deciding together

A four column grid of open questions, numbered 01 to 04, placed after the brief and before the body. It puts the unknowns in the client's court early rather than surfacing them as problems later.

What would be most useful at the review

Tell me what you do not want. The colors to stay away from, the words that feel wrong, the references that miss. That is faster and more useful than telling me what works.

Parking lot

Numbered list of what was raised and set aside, each with a reason and a tag for where it came from. Framed as parked, not rejected. Protects scope without reading as a series of refusals.
